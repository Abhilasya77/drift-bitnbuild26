Is the temporary credential a government ID?

No. It's a platform-issued, time-limited credential that participating services can choose to accept. It doesn't replace a passport, national ID or any official document.

My name is written differently on each document. Is that a problem?

Usually not. Spacing, ordering, abbreviations and transliteration are recognised automatically and recorded as explainable variants. Only genuinely different names go to a person for review.

What happens if my case needs human review?

You'll see a calm “Under review” status with the exact field in question. If more evidence helps, you'll be asked for one specific document — not your whole file again.

Do you store my face scan?

We keep the verification result and a reference — passed, when, and how — not the raw biometric data behind it.

What happens when my permanent ID arrives?

Upload it and it's checked against your existing identity record. If it's consistent, it's linked and your temporary credential expires automatically.
